# About Me:
I’m Aryan, a 14-year-old student and aspiring software engineer currently learning the fundamentals of programming and building real projects. My long-term goal is to work in big tech and become a highly capable engineer in whatever area I choose to specialise in, whether that’s AI, web development, app development, or something completely new. I’m focused on building strong foundations early so I can grow into a well-rounded developer.<br><br>I’m currently learning web development — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — and gradually moving toward more advanced tools, languages, and frameworks. I code on a MacBook Air (M4) using VS Code and Git/GitHub for version control. My only early experience was experimenting with Scratch back in primary school, but now I’m fully focused on proper development and real-world coding practices.<br><br>My goals are to build a solid portfolio, strengthen my problem-solving skills, and keep improving academically and technically. I’m committed to consistency and long-term growth, pushing myself step by step toward the skill level needed to succeed in major tech companies.<br><br>Outside of coding, I’m deeply interested in tech, the future, and building the kind of life I want for myself. I’m motivated by the idea of achieving financial success and eventually owning some of the dream cars I’ve loved since childhood, such as the Ferrari F40, Koenigsegg Agera RS, BMW E36 M3, Porsche 911 GT2 RS, and especially the Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 and Toyota Supra MK4 from Fast & Furious.
